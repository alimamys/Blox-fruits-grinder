# "11 of Us Went Camping. 12 Came Back." — production plan

The script, in our own words, is in [`SCRIPT.md`](SCRIPT.md): about 1,380 spoken words, roughly 10:20.

## Source and credit

- **Source:** a new retelling of the key events of "Anansi's Goatman Story", an anonymous post on 4chan /x/ (28 September 2012), as archived on the Creepypasta Wiki.
- **What changed:**
  - characters renamed;
  - restructured around a cold-open hook;
  - rewritten in our own words;
  - cleaned up for YouTube (no drinking, light language).
- **Credit (on screen and in the description):**
  > Based on "Anansi's Goatman Story" by Anansi (4chan /x/, 2012), Creepypasta Wiki.
- **Rights:** the original author never came forward, so nobody can grant permission. Monetised retellings of this story are common, but legally it is a grey area.

## The look

- **Same as the last two films:**
  - images from ChatGPT's image model (gpt-image-2) in the semi-realistic 2D style;
  - voices from Fish Audio Drama 3;
  - animation in code, with blinks, extra drawings, lip sync, camera moves and light flicker.
- **Rule for the monster:** we never show it clearly; the film works by implication. The thing appears only as:
  - figures in the trees with their backs turned;
  - something jerking between trunks in a flashlight beam;
  - the "Nia" by the fire pit, from behind, laughing without sound;
  - the girl on the trail with a slack-jawed smile;
  - a low shape crawling out of the brush;
  - a silhouette lying on the trailer floor;
  - one shadowy goat-headed outline for Ray's legend, half lost in the dark.

## Cast (character sheets)

1. **Marcus**, the narrator: 16, Black, a city kid from Chicago. A turnaround plus an expressions board.
2. **Wyatt Dawes:** 16, white, skinny country kid; later carries a rifle.
3. **The cousins:**
   - Dez, 17, big and tough;
   - Ray, 15, the little cousin who knows the legend;
   - Nia, 16, the girl it touched.
4. **The others:** six more teenagers, three boys and three girls, so the group is exactly 11.
5. **The adults:** Mr. Dawes, Wyatt's dad (50s, with a shotgun), and Cole, Wyatt's 19-year-old cousin (lantern and rifle).
6. **The thing:**
   - the wrong-Nia: same clothes, grey pallor, slack jaw, too still;
   - the goat-headed silhouette for the legend;
   - the low crawling shape.

## Locations

1. The clearing: two old camping trailers and a fire pit in pine woods, drawn by day, at dusk and at night.
2. Inside the trailer: a cramped living room, a kitchenette with a stove, the bathroom door, a window with blinds.
3. The forest trail at night, seen by flashlight.
4. The creek by day.
5. The Dawes farmyard: a pigpen with a broken fence. The pig is implied, never shown.

## Voices (Fish Drama 3)

- **Narration:** Marcus, a young adult voice telling it years later.
- **Characters:** Marcus at 16, Wyatt, Ray, Nia, Mr. Dawes and Cole.
- **The voice at the door:** pitched, halting and robotic, built with a new "no cadence" effect.
