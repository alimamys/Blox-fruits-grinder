# "Mr. Widemouth" — production plan

The script, in our own words, is in [`SCRIPT.md`](SCRIPT.md): about 1,290 spoken words, roughly 9:40 of story and about 10:00 with the title and visual holds.

## Source and credit

- **Source:** a new retelling of the key events of "Mr. Widemouth" by **PerfectCircle35** (Creepypasta.com, 7 November 2010; archived on the Creepypasta Wiki).
- **What changed:**
  - the story opens on its most chilling image (the wave with the steak knife);
  - the boy and his family are named, and the text is in our own words;
  - two new scares: waking at the open window ("Almost."), and the missing steak knife;
  - a new present-day ending: his own son, five years old, has a new friend under the bed.
- **Credit (end card and description):**
  > Based on "Mr. Widemouth" by PerfectCircle35 (Creepypasta.com, 2010).
- **Rights:** the author is known and was not asked. This retelling is a derivative work, so the author could file a claim against a monetised video. The user chose to go ahead on that basis.

## The look

- **Same pipeline as the last two films:** gpt-image-2 frames in the semi-realistic 2D style, Fish Drama 3 voices (narrator A), animation in code, the count-style motif where it fits, and mystery thumbnails.
- **The creature is seen, but small and sweet at first.** About the size of a cat, with a too-big round head, shiny black eyes, crooked ears and a mouth from ear to ear. It turns subtly wrong as the film goes on: the snarl, the grin at the mattress edge, the silhouette in the window. It is never gory.
- **The child is never shown hurt.** The danger is implied (the window, the box of knives, the trail), and the film is not made for kids.

## Cast (character sheets)

1. **Eli, age 5:** small, pale and feverish, in striped pyjamas. A turnaround plus an expressions board.
2. **Mr. Widemouth:** the creature's design sheet (front, side, under the bed, the snarl).
3. **Mom and Dad:** early 30s, tired, kind, in 1990s clothes.
4. **Adult Eli (30) and his son Leo (5):** the present day.

## Locations

1. The farmhouse on the hill, in rural Maine, in spring (day, dusk, and 4 a.m.).
2. Eli's bedroom: bare except the bed, moving boxes, picture books and ginger ale.
3. The empty room at the end of the hall: bare floorboards and one tall window, with the long drop down the hill outside.
4. The upstairs hallway.
5. The back porch and the deer trail into the woods.
6. The old cemetery at the end of the trail, with small headstones.
7. Inside the moving truck's cab at 4 a.m.
8. The burned-out stone foundation, years later.
9. Leo's bedroom today.

## Voices (Fish Drama 3)

- **Adult Eli (narrator):** the narrator from the last film (A), so the channel has one recognisable voice.
- **Eli at 5, and Leo:** child voices.
- **Mr. Widemouth:** sweet and sing-song, turning flat. A light pitch shift and the `thing`-style rhythm wobble on his last lines.
- **Mom and Dad:** warm adult voices.

## Steps

1. Look-dev: character sheets and locations.
2. Voices and QA.
3. **First minute** (the hook, the title and the empty house), sent to you for approval.
4. The rest of the film.
5. Render and checks: contact sheets, loudness, and a review of every frame.
6. YouTube package: 3 mystery thumbnails, title, description with the credit, chapters and tags.
