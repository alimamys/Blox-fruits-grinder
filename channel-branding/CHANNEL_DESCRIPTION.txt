Bedtime stories... just not the kind you tell your kids.

Daddy Tales is a channel of animated horror stories, told the way a dad tells them at bedtime: the lights low, the voice quiet, and something waiting at the end. An imaginary friend who wants you to jump. A knock on the door at exactly 3:06 a.m. A camping trip where the headcount stops adding up.

Some are classic creepypastas retold in our own words, always credited to their authors. Some are originals. Each one is about ten minutes long, fully animated, with sound built for headphones.

Headphones on. Lights off. Turn the page.

Subscribe and turn on notifications so you never miss a new tale.

All stories are fiction, made for teen and adult audiences - not for young children.
