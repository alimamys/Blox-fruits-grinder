CHANNEL DESCRIPTION (Customization > Basic info > Description)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Some stories are meant for after bedtime.

Past Bedtime Tales is animated horror for the hours when the house goes quiet. A knock on the door at exactly 3:06 a.m. An imaginary friend who lives under the bed. A camping trip where the headcount stops adding up. A frame in an animation that nobody drew.

Every story is told in the first person and animated scene by scene, with sound built for headphones. Some are originals; some are classic creepypastas retold in our own words, always credited to their writers.

A new story every week, with short scares in between.

Headphones on. Lights off. Don't look under the bed.

All stories are fiction, made for teens and adults - not for young children.
