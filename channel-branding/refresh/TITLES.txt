Why they no longer look alike: each has its own layout, typography (UI sans / LED clock + typewriter /
handwriting / book serif), colours and "object" (a screen, a door, a photo, a book), made from the film's
own frames with a softer printed or photographed finish - no shared template, no red circles or arrows,
no new generated art. Titles each use a different sentence shape and none ends in "| Animated Horror Story"
(keep "animated horror story" in the tags and the first line of each description instead).
