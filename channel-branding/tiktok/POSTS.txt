HOW TO POST
- One part a day (or two a day 6+ hours apart), always in order, at roughly the same time.
- After posting, add every part to a TikTok PLAYLIST called "Mr. Widemouth" (Profile > Playlists), so a viewer who
  finds part 3 can binge from part 1. (Playlists need the feature on your account; if you don't have it yet,
  pin part 1 to your profile instead.)
- Pin a comment on each part: "Part N+1 is up on my profile" once the next part is out.
- Cover: pick a frame where a face fills the picture (TikTok lets you choose the cover frame when posting),
  and add the cover text "PART N" so the grid reads in order.
- Sound: original sound. Do not add trending music on top of the voices.
- Label: if TikTok asks, "AI-generated content" -> on (the art was made with an image model).
