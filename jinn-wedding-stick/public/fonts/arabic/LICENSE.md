# Arabic fonts (newspaper page)

GPL-2 fonts from Debian packages, used to draw the 1997 Kuwaiti-style newspaper page:

- `ae_Tholoth.ttf` - Arabeyes project (fonts-arabeyes 2.1), GPL-2 - the thuluth masthead
- `KacstPoster.ttf`, `KacstBook.ttf`, `KacstOffice.ttf` - King Abdulaziz City for Science and Technology
  (fonts-kacst 2.01), GPL-2 - headlines, body text, small print
