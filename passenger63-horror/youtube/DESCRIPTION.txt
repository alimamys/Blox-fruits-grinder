At 12:17 in the morning, the crew of the North Star ferry counted the passengers for the third time.

The manifest said 62.
There were 63.

An old woman in a black coat was sitting alone on the rear deck, staring out at the ocean. No ticket. No name on the list.
"I'm not a passenger," she said. "He shouldn't have come back."

The 63rd Passenger is an animated horror story about a night crossing, a name that disappears, and the one passenger who was never supposed to be counted.
